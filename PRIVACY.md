# NotiVault Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

NotiVault keeps a private record of the notifications you receive so you can read messages later, including
ones the sender deleted.

## What NotiVault accesses
- **Notification content** (via Android's notification access, granted by you): app name, sender, conversation
  name, message text, timestamps and preview images, from the apps you have not excluded.
- **Media folders you choose** (optional, via Android's folder picker): read-only access to the specific
  folders you select, such as WhatsApp's media folder, so photos, videos and voice notes can be kept.

## Where it goes
Nowhere. All data is stored **only on your device**, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.
NotiVault does not request the internet permission, so it cannot upload, sync, sell or share your data.
There are no analytics, ads or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data leaves the device only when **you** explicitly export it: a receipt image or PDF, a chat transcript,
or a password-protected `.nvault` backup that you save or share yourself.

## Your controls
Exclude any app · pause recording (in-app or via the Quick Settings tile) · auto-delete after 30/90/365 days ·
erase everything in Settings · uninstalling removes all data. Android backup is disabled for this app.

## Use responsibly
NotiVault is for reading messages sent **to you** on **your own** device. Do not install it on someone else's
device to monitor them.
